Our story

From 12 bikes to a citywide network

CycleFlow started with a group of neighbors who were tired of waiting for buses that never came.

In 2019 we pooled donations, refurbished a dozen bikes and opened one station in Union Square. Riders showed up from day one. We added stations wherever people asked for them, and we still plan new ones that way, through community meetings and rider requests.

Today we're a nonprofit operator with a fleet of classic bikes and e-bikes, a maintenance crew that services every bike every two weeks, and a reduced-fare program that keeps riding open to everyone.

Mission & vision

Why we keep pedaling

What we do every day, and the city we're working toward.

Our mission

Make getting around affordable, healthy and low-carbon for every neighbor.

We put bikes where people need them, keep them in top shape and price them so the trip costs less than a bus fare.

Our vision

A city where a bike is always a five-minute walk away.

We picture streets with fewer cars, cleaner air and safer lanes, where bike share connects every neighborhood to transit, work and school.

Access for all

Reduced fares and stations in every neighborhood.

Safety first

Bikes inspected every two weeks, lights on every ride.

Planet positive

Community-led
