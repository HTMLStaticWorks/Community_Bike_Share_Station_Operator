FAQ

Support questions, answered

Quick answers before you reach out.

How quickly will I hear back?

Phone and live chat are answered in a couple of minutes, 24/7. Messages sent through this form or by email get a reply within one business day, and usually the same day.

I think I was charged incorrectly. What should I do?

Pick "Billing & membership" above and include the ride date and station. We check the dock logs and refund any minutes after your bike was docked, usually within 48 hours.

Will I be charged if I report a damaged bike?

No. Report it within 3 minutes of unlocking and the ride is free. You can report it in the app, on the dock, or here using the bike's 5-digit ID.

How do I request a new station?

Send us the intersection or address with the topic "Station issue or request". We review requests every quarter, and the most-requested spots go to community planning meetings.

Do you offer memberships for companies or schools?

Yes. We offer group rates from 10 members and can sponsor a station near your building. Choose "Partnerships & sponsorship" and our partnerships team will be in touch.
