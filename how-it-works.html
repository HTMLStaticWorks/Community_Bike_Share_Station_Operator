Bike return instructions

Dock it right, every time.

Your ride only ends once the dock locks the bike. Follow these five steps and you'll never pay for minutes you didn't ride.

  1. Find a dock with a green light

    Open docks glow green. Check the station map before you arrive to see how many are free.

  2. Roll the front wheel in straight

    Line the bike up with the dock and push firmly until the front fork clicks into the lock.

  3. Wait for the beep and solid green

    A short beep and a steady green light mean the bike is locked. It takes about two seconds.

  4. Give the bike a gentle tug

    If it stays put, you're done. If it slides out, push it back in and wait for the beep again.

  5. Check for "Ride ended" in your dashboard

    Your receipt with ride time and cost appears within a minute. Keep it if you plan to dispute a charge.

What to do if a station is full

No open dock? No problem.

It happens at busy stations during rush hour. You won't be charged for the extra minutes it takes to find another dock.

  1. Tap "Station full" in your active ride

    Or press the help button on any dock at the station. Either way, it logs your arrival time.

  2. Get 15 free minutes

    We add 15 minutes of ride time to your trip at no charge, so there's no rush.

  3. Follow directions to the nearest open dock

    The dashboard shows the closest stations with free docks and the quickest route to each.

  4. Still stuck? Call rider support

    At big events we run valet docking. Our 24/7 team will tell you where to leave the bike.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about riding with CycleFlow.

Can I return a bike to a different station?

Yes. You can dock at any of our 200+ stations, not just the one you started from. If that station is full, see what to do if a station is full.

How do I report a damaged bike?

Before undocking or immediately after, hit the "Report Issue" button in your active ride dashboard. You will not be charged if reported within 3 minutes.

How long can I keep the bike?

Most plans include unlimited 45-minute rides. Keeping the bike out longer incurs overage fees to ensure bikes remain available for everyone.

Can I pause my ride?

Currently, you cannot pause a ride while keeping the bike out. You must dock the bike to end your session, but you can instantly unlock another one.
