Step by step

From sign-up to first ride

Three steps, a few minutes, and you're rolling.

Step 01

Sign up & find a station

Create your CycleFlow account in seconds and pick pay-as-you-go or an unlimited pass. Then open the live station map to see bikes and open docks near you.

  • Account ready in under a minute
  • Real-time bike & dock availability
Step 02

Scan, unlock & ride

At the station, scan the QR code or enter the 5-digit bike code in your dashboard. Adjust the seat, then take the scenic route or the fast lane.

  • Unlocks in about 3 seconds
  • Classic bikes and e-bikes available
Step 03

Return & auto-bill

Push the bike firmly into any empty dock. Wait for the green light and beep. Your ride ends and billing happens automatically.

  • Dock at any of 200+ stations
  • Receipt sent to your dashboard

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about riding with CycleFlow.

What if the station is full?

If you arrive at a full station, you can request an extra 15 minutes of free ride time via your dashboard to get to the nearest open station.

How do I report a damaged bike?

Before undocking or immediately after, hit the "Report Issue" button in your active ride dashboard. You will not be charged if reported within 3 minutes.

How long can I keep the bike?

Most plans include unlimited 45-minute rides. Keeping the bike out longer incurs overage fees to ensure bikes remain available for everyone.

Can I pause my ride?

Currently, you cannot pause a ride while keeping the bike out. You must dock the bike to end your session, but you can instantly unlock another one.
