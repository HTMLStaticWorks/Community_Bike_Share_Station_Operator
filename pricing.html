FAQ

Pricing questions, answered

Everything you need to know before choosing a plan.

Can I switch or cancel my plan anytime?

Yes. You can upgrade, downgrade or cancel from your dashboard at any time. Changes take effect at the start of your next billing cycle, and there are no cancellation fees.

What happens if my ride goes over the included time?

Rides beyond your plan's included time are billed per minute: $0.15/min on the Monthly Commuter plan after 45 minutes, and $0.10/min on the Annual Pass after 60 minutes. Dock and unlock a new bike to start a fresh ride.

Do e-bikes cost extra?

E-bike upgrades are included with the Annual Pass. On Pay-As-You-Go and Monthly plans, e-bikes are available for a small per-minute surcharge shown in the app before you unlock.

How and when am I charged?

Memberships are billed upfront to the card on your account. Pay-As-You-Go rides and any overage fees are charged automatically when you dock, and a receipt appears in your dashboard.
